{% if kind=='how' %}

1. Discover a real rental

We promote currently available, manager-authorized properties. The manager controls the leasing process.

2. Choose how to apply

You can contact the property manager directly. If you choose our optional $24.99 service, we collect your information through one secure application and forward it to that manager.

Two ways we can help

Application submission. Where a property manager accepts our packets, we forward your application through the agreed process and confirm receipt. This is only offered for properties where that delivery path is verified.

Application assistance. Independent help preparing your application and guiding you through the manager's own official application process. You submit the finished packet yourself through the manager's route. Assistance does not file the application for you, does not guarantee the manager will accept it, and is not proof it was submitted.

3. Get confirmation

Payments are processed by Stripe. We only mark an application paid after server-side confirmation and delivered after confirmed fulfillment.

{% elif kind=='faq' %}

Do I have to use your service?

No. You may contact the property manager directly. The $24.99 charge is for our independent application collection and forwarding service.

What does the fee cover?

Application intake, secure payment handling and forwarding to the named participating manager. It does not cover the manager's separate screening or application fees, if any.

What is the difference between submission and assistance?

Submission means we forward your application to a manager who has agreed to receive our packets, and we confirm receipt. Assistance means we help you prepare your application and walk you through the manager's own official application process — you submit the finished packet yourself. Assistance does not file the application for you and is not proof the manager accepted it.

Will I be approved?

We cannot promise approval. The property manager makes that decision.

Can I see the property before paying?

Yes. Use the viewing contact or direct manager link provided on the listing where available.

What if the manager can't receive the application?

If the promised service cannot be delivered before submission, our policy is to provide a refund of our service fee.
